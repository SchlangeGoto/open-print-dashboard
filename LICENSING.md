- Use it for paid consulting or support work for a licensee.

## What you cannot do

- Offer the dashboard, or a modified version, as a commercial product or

- Remove or hide the license, copyright or trademark notices.
- Use the OpenPrintDash name or logo to promote a fork.

The restriction is a **Competing Use** restriction. It is not a general
non-commercial restriction: companies may run it internally, and consultants
may charge for helping someone who is entitled to use it.

## It becomes Apache-2.0

Every version released under FSL converts to the Apache License 2.0 on the
second anniversary of the date that version was made available. At that point
the restrictions above no longer apply to that version.

This is why the license is described as "eventually open source". It is **not
OSI-approved open source while the FSL terms apply**. The site and README should
say "source-available", not "open source", for current releases.

want to offer a hosted or embedded version, contact the maintainer instead of
